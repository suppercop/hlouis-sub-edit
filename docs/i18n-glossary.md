# HLouis Sub Edit — EN → VI Fansub Terminology Glossary

Consistent Vietnamese localization conventions used across `crates/i18n-catalog/locales/{en,vi}.ftl` and `docs/user/vi/`. Terms widely used in English by Vietnamese fansubbers (`Karaoke`, `Typeset`, `Style`, `Keyframe`, `Timecode`, `MKV`, `SRT`, `ASS`) are intentionally kept in English for clarity and Aegisub 3.2.2/3.5.1 familiarity.
